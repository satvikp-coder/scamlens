## 1. What ScamLens does

ScamLens helps you understand suspicious messages and links. You paste text or a link into the app and it shows warning signs found by built-in rules.

## 2. Information we collect

**We do not collect your information.** The messages and links you check are analysed on your phone and are **not sent to us or to anyone else**. ScamLens does not have the Internet permission, has no user accounts, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

## 3. Information stored on your phone

To work, the app stores the following **only on your device**:

- your language choice and a few settings;
- your practice-quiz score;
- if "Save a summary of my checks" is on (it is on by default): for each check, the date/time, whether it was a message or a link, the risk level, and the names of the warning signs found (and small details used to explain them, such as a look-alike website name). The text you checked is **not** saved unless you turn on "Also save the original text".

This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it. You can delete one entry, delete all history, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Automatic cloud backup of this data is turned off.

## 4. Permissions

ScamLens does not request access to your SMS, contacts, call log, notifications, location, camera, microphone, storage or accessibility services, and has no Internet permission. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste".

## 5. Sharing

ScamLens does not share your information with third parties. If you use "Share summary", the short summary (risk level and names of the warning signs, not your original message) is handed to the app you choose; that app's own policy applies.

## 6. Children

ScamLens is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## 7. Security

Data stays on your device in the app's private storage. No method is perfectly secure; for example, someone with access to your unlocked phone could open the app and see your history. You can turn history off.

## 8. Your choices

Turn history off or delete it in Settings at any time. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a server.

## 9. Changes

If we ever add features that change this (for example an online link-reputation lookup), we will update this policy and the app description **before** release and ask for your choice.
