# Content rating and target audience — guidance (not submitted)

These are suggestions for the owner. The questionnaires in Play Console are declarations you make yourself; answer them truthfully for the exact build you upload.

## App category

- Suggested: **Tools** (or **Education**). It is an awareness/checking tool, not a financial service.
- Not a financial app: it does not handle money, loans or payments.

## Target audience and content

- Suggested target age group: **18 and over**. The app explains fraud tactics that involve money, and it is not designed for children. Choosing adults only avoids the "Designed for Families" requirements. If you later target younger teens, review Google's Families policy first.
- Not appealing to children as a primary audience: no cartoon characters, games for kids, etc.
